# Caffeine Molecular Targets

## Summary

At normal coffee-consumption concentrations in humans, caffeine is best modeled as a [non-selective antagonist](pharmacology-vocabulary.md) of [adenosine receptors](adenosine-receptors.md). At higher concentrations, it can also inhibit [phosphodiesterases](secondary-caffeine-targets.md), activate [ryanodine receptors](secondary-caffeine-targets.md), inhibit [ATM/ATR](secondary-caffeine-targets.md), and inhibit [mTOR-linked growth pathways](secondary-caffeine-targets.md).

Upstream of human pharmacology, caffeine is a [plant purine alkaloid](purine-alkaloids.md) and [methylxanthine](xanthines.md). That matters because the molecule's evolutionary role is ecological perturbation, while the project's human biology focus is a downstream consequence of conserved purine/xanthine chemistry.

## Physiological Target Class

The main physiological target class is [adenosine receptors](adenosine-receptors.md):

- [ADORA1](adenosine-receptors.md)
- [ADORA2A](adenosine-receptors.md)
- [ADORA2B](adenosine-receptors.md)
- [ADORA3](adenosine-receptors.md)

Blocking these receptors changes intracellular [cAMP](camp-signaling.md) and downstream transcriptional programs in a cell-type-specific way.

For the full cAMP cascade, see [cAMP signaling and ADORA cascades](camp-signaling.md).

## Higher-Concentration Targets

These targets matter, but interpretation requires dose awareness:

- [PDE inhibition](secondary-caffeine-targets.md): prolongs cAMP/cGMP signaling, but the proposal places this around roughly 500 uM to 1 mM.
- [Ryanodine receptor activation](secondary-caffeine-targets.md): increases calcium release, often studied at supraphysiological concentrations.
- [ATM/ATR inhibition](secondary-caffeine-targets.md): affects DNA damage checkpoint signaling.
- [mTOR inhibition](secondary-caffeine-targets.md): links caffeine to autophagy and cell growth regulation.

## Insects and Plants

Do not assume every organism uses the same target hierarchy. In mammals, physiological caffeine is mostly [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md) antagonism. In invertebrates, [ryanodine receptor and phosphodiesterase effects](secondary-caffeine-targets.md) are well supported, while direct adenosine-receptor pharmacology is more nuanced. In plants, caffeine toxicity and allelopathy are real areas of study, but the molecular mechanism should be handled cautiously.

## Why This Matters for Epigenomics

The target determines which transcription factors and chromatin regulators are plausible. [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md) blockade points first to [cAMP](camp-signaling.md)/PKA/CREB and receptor-specific G-protein logic. [RyR](secondary-caffeine-targets.md) and calcium signaling point to CaMKII, HDAC export, MEF2, and NFAT. [mTOR](secondary-caffeine-targets.md) points to autophagy and growth-state programs.
