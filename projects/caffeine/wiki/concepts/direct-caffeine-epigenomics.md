# Direct Caffeine Epigenomics

## Summary

Direct caffeine-treatment epigenomics is the bottleneck. The proposal identifies endothelial datasets as the strongest direct evidence, while many other caffeine findings are gene-specific, expression-only, methylation-association-only, or high-dose pathway studies.

## Anchor Datasets

Findley et al. 2019:

- Human umbilical vein endothelial cells.
- Paired RNA-seq and ATAC-seq.
- Caffeine treatment across 17 donors.
- Differential accessibility and expression after treatment.
- Motif results included INSM1, PLAG1, and ZNF423.

Boye et al. 2024:

- BiT-STARR-Seq reporter assay.
- Tested many variants for caffeine-response regulatory effects.
- NFAT family factors were enriched among differentially active targets.

## Other Evidence Types

- EWAS studies connect coffee or caffeine intake to methylation sites, mostly in blood or cord blood.
- Gene-specific histone work links caffeine, calcium signaling, HDAC5 export, MEF2, and GLUT4 promoter acetylation in muscle cells.
- Some inflammatory and autophagy studies report pathway effects but often at high caffeine concentrations.

## What Is Missing

- No genome-wide histone ChIP-seq after caffeine treatment.
- No caffeine Hi-C or promoter-capture data.
- No single-cell caffeine multiome.
- No systematic time-course ATAC-seq.
