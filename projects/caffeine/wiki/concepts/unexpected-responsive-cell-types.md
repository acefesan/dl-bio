# Unexpected Responsive Cell Types

## Summary

The proposal argues that caffeine responsiveness likely extends beyond canonical brain, liver, heart, and endocrine contexts.

## Candidate Cell Types

| Cell type | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Oligodendrocyte precursor cells | Express adenosine receptors; adenosine promotes differentiation and myelination |
| Osteoblasts and osteoclasts | Dose-dependent caffeine effects on bone formation and resorption pathways |
| Spermatocytes | Adenosine receptor and [PDE](secondary-caffeine-targets.md)-related effects on sperm motility and genomic integrity |
| Vascular smooth muscle cells | [ADORA2A](adenosine-receptors.md)/cAMP/CREB/RUNX2 axis may affect vascular calcification |
| Gut epithelial and microbiome-linked contexts | [ADORA2B](adenosine-receptors.md) and microbiome associations suggest gut relevance |
| Skin cells and melanocytes | Oxidative stress, A2A, SIRT3/AMPK, autophagy, and melanoma-selective effects |

## Project Use

These cell types are useful sanity checks. If the model only recovers expected CNS and liver biology, it may be too narrow. If it prioritizes these unexpected cell types through receptor expression, accessibility, or TF activity, the model is capturing the proposal's broader hypothesis.
