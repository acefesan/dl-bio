# Research Questions

## Beginner

1. Which human cell types express adenosine receptors at highest levels?
2. What are the tissue-specific eQTL effects of caffeine GWAS SNPs?
3. What gene sets are enriched among caffeine GWAS loci?
4. What is caffeine's transcriptomic signature in LINCS L1000?
5. How does CYP1A2 expression vary across liver cell subtypes?

## Intermediate

6. Do caffeine GWAS variants overlap enhancers active in unexpected cell types?
7. What WGCNA co-expression modules contain [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md) genes across tissues?
8. What TF regulons are active in [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md)-high cell types?
9. Can chromatin accessibility at [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md) loci predict receptor expression across cell types?
10. How does the NFAT TF family respond to caffeine across vascular cell types?
11. Do caffeine-associated CpGs overlap tissue-specific regulatory elements?
12. Is there genetic pleiotropy between caffeine consumption and bone mineral density?

## Advanced

13. Can CellOracle predict cell-type-specific transcriptomic responses to adenosine receptor blockade?
14. What are the PheWAS signatures of caffeine-metabolizing enzyme variants?
15. Can multi-omics integration reveal how caffeine GWAS variants affect chromatin in immune cells?
16. Can network pharmacology predict caffeine's effects on the tumor immune microenvironment?
17. How does genetic variation in caffeine metabolism interact with developing-brain expression programs?
18. What is the 3D chromatin context of caffeine GWAS variants at [ADORA](adenosine-receptors.md) loci?
19. Can a multi-tissue caffeine response model identify kidney-function mediating pathways?
20. Can a graph neural network predict cell-type-specific caffeine responses from molecular features?

## Recommended Sequence

Start with Q1, then Q9, then Q10 or Q13. This keeps the project grounded in receptor biology before moving into high-dimensional perturbation modeling.
