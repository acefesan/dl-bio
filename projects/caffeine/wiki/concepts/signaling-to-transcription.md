# Signaling to Transcription

## Summary

Caffeine becomes an epigenomics question when receptor and stress signaling converge on transcription factors, chromatin modifiers, and enhancer activity. The core task is to translate pathway perturbation into predicted regulatory-element changes.

For the broken-down cAMP cascade downstream of ADORA receptors, see [cAMP signaling and ADORA cascades](camp-signaling.md).

## Main Cascades

| Cascade | Regulatory endpoint | Epigenomic readout to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| [cAMP](camp-signaling.md)/PKA/CREB | CREB phosphorylation, CBP/p300 recruitment | CREB motif accessibility, H3K27ac, expression of CREB targets |
| [PDE inhibition](secondary-caffeine-targets.md) | sustained cAMP/cGMP | prolonged PKA/PKG targets, dose-sensitive interpretation |
| [RyR](secondary-caffeine-targets.md)/calcium/CaMKII | HDAC5 export, MEF2 activation, NFAT activity | histone acetylation, MEF2/NFAT motifs, calcium-response genes |
| MAPK/ERK | CREB/AP-1/Nrf2 effects depending on context | AP-1 motifs, immediate early genes, enhancer activation |
| PI3K/Akt/[mTOR](secondary-caffeine-targets.md) | autophagy and growth programs | pathway gene expression, chromatin at growth/autophagy genes |
| NF-kB | inflammatory cytokine programs | RELA/NF-kB motifs, inflammatory enhancers |
| Nrf2/ARE | antioxidant-defense genes | NFE2L2 motifs, ARE-driven expression |

## Important Caution

Several non-receptor effects are reported at millimolar caffeine concentrations, which are far above typical plasma levels from normal consumption. When interpreting direct datasets, always track concentration and exposure duration.
